Gecko - Privacy Policy

Gecko is a personal app used only by its developer, on their own computer.

What data it accesses

With my permission, Gecko reads my Google Calendar, Google Classroom and Gmail data. It uses this data only to show me my schedule, assignments and study reminders.

How data is stored and shared

Data is stored only on my own computer. Gecko does not sell, share or publish Google user data, and no one else has access to it. Parts of my data may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic) only to answer my own questions inside the app.

Revoking access

I can sign out in the app, or remove Gecko's access at any time in my Google Account under Security > Third-party access.
